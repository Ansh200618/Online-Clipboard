How Copy Cloud Works

Move text and files between phone, laptop, tablet or desktop in three steps - without accounts, cables or messenger apps.

1. Open the Send tab

On the device that has the content, open copycloud.me. Paste your text into the box or drag & drop the file you want to share. Copy Cloud instantly encrypts the transfer and stores it in temporary cloud storage.

2. Get your 6-character code and QR

You get a short, human-readable code like A1B2C3 plus a QR that encodes the same code as a deep link. Either share the code with someone or scan the QR with your other device.

3. Retrieve on any device

On the receiving device, open Copy Cloud, go to the Retrieve tab, and type the 6 characters. If you scanned the QR, the code is filled and the content is fetched automatically. Text lands in your clipboard, files download to your default folder.

Automatic 24-hour cleanup

Every clip self-destructs after 24 hours. No history, no accounts, no leftovers - just a clean pipe between your devices when you need it.
